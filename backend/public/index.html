Common Questions

Can stolen crypto actually be recovered?

Confirmed blockchain transactions generally cannot be reversed. An exchange or authority may be able to review a case, but no one can guarantee recovery.

How much does an investigation cost?

Ask for the full scope and fees in writing before agreeing to any work or sending money. Be cautious of unexpected advance or unlock fees.

What do you need from me to begin?

A public transaction hash and a short description may be enough to begin a discussion. Never send a seed phrase, private key, or password.

Blockchain Investigation — Method Overview

Abstract

Public blockchains record confirmed transactions, which generally cannot be reversed. Reviewing those records may help explain where funds moved, but does not guarantee that assets can be returned.

1. The Irreversibility Problem

Crypto-asset losses fall into two categories: unauthorised transfers (theft) and accidental transfers. Neither is reversible at the protocol level. Recovery is therefore an investigative and legal process.

2. Reviewing public transaction data

Investigators may review transaction inputs and outputs, timing, public contract interactions, and known bridge activity. Address attribution is uncertain and should not be treated as proof of identity.

3. Honest Caveats

No firm can guarantee recovery. Outcomes depend on the circumstances and, where relevant, the decisions of exchanges, custodians, and authorities.
