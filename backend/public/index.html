ChainRecover Whitepaper — v1.2

Abstract

Public blockchains are permanent ledgers. Once a transaction is confirmed it cannot be reversed — yet value is rarely truly gone; it is only moved. This paper describes the methodology ChainRecover uses to follow that movement.

1. The Irreversibility Problem

Crypto-asset losses fall into two categories: unauthorised transfers (theft) and accidental transfers. Neither is reversible at the protocol level. Recovery is therefore an investigative and legal process.

2. The Six-Layer Tracing Model

We map every input and output, use timing and gas patterns to group addresses, resolve cross-chain bridge hops, detect off-ramps, and package it into a court-ready report.

3. Honest Caveats

No firm can guarantee recovery. Outcomes depend on the cooperation of exchanges and the speed of law enforcement. We decline cases we believe are not recoverable.
